CRYPTOGRAPHY POLICIES
---------------------
Below are the cryptography policies used in this application:

(1) Dependencies
	* OpenSSL (`openssl`)
	* Tarball (`tar`)
	* XZ Compressor (`xz`)
	* Gunzip Compressor (`gunzip`)
(2) Method
	* Encryption-then-MAC (EtM)
		* ARGON2ID + CHACHA20 <-> HMAC-SHA256
(3) Password
	* ARGON2ID
		* Minimum characters: 32
		* Must have at least one(1) 'A-Z'
		* Must have at least one(1) 'a-z'
		* Must have at least one(1) '0-9'
		* Must have at least one(1) '#$%&@^`~\\/\|_-'
		* 'Salt':
			* minimum length: '32'
			* default length: '32'
			* length nature: even number (dividable by 2)
		* 'Hash Iteration':
			* minimum: '2' (OWASP)
			* default: '3'
		* 'Memory Size' in kB:
			* minimum: '19456' (19 * 1024) (OWASP)
			* default: '26624' (26 * 1024)
		* 'Parallelism':
			* minimum: '1' (OWASP)
			* default: '1'
(4) Encryption:
	* CHACHA20
(5) MAC:
	* HMAC-SHA256 (default)
		* using SHA-256 algorithm.
	* HMAC-SHA512
		* using SHA-512 algorithm
	* HMAC-SHA512-256
		* using SHA-512-256 algorith,
(6) SHASUM:
	* SHA256 (default)
	* SHA512
	* SHA512-256
(7) Archive:
	* tar
(8) Compression:
	* xz
	* gzip
(9) Encapsulation:
	*	Format:
			-----BEGIN PRIVATE PAYLOAD SIGNATURE-----\n
			Method: <HMAC type>\n
			\n
			\n
			<HMAC digest>\n
			-----END PRIVATE PAYLOAD SIGNATURE-----\n
			-----BEGIN PRIVATE PAYLOAD-----\n
			Method: <ID>\n
			Encryption: <ID>\n
			Salt: <hex>\n
			Iteration: <number>\n
			Memory-Size: <number>\n
			Parallelism: <number>\n
			...
			\n
			\n
			<base64 ciphertext>\n
			-----END PRIVATE PAYLOAD-----\n
	*	Format is **ALWAYS** favor decryption needs.
	*	All data **MUST** be Base64 encoded when needed.
	*	`HMAC digest` **MUST** be UPPERCASE with colon separator
		(e.g. `AA:BB:CC:...`).
	*	`SIGNATURE` section is optional. Should it be available,
		it **MUST** be the first section.
	*	Standards are based on OpenPGP ASCII Armor (RFC 4880 §6) wearing
		PEM's delimiters.
